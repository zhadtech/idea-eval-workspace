# Criteria

The judging standard. `scripts/score.mjs` reads the tables in this file. Keep the table shapes when you edit.

## Criteria and weights
Score each criterion 1–5. Weights add up to 100.

| ID | Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| C01 | Demand and buying power | 20 |
| C02 | Profit potential | 15 |
| C03 | Scales without your labor or face | 15 |
| C04 | Cost and time to first output | 15 |
| C05 | Competition and your edge | 10 |
| C06 | Doable with your skills plus AI | 10 |
| C07 | Builds your value | 10 |
| C08 | Good for people | 5 |

## Anchors (what 1, 3, and 5 mean)
- **C01 Demand and buying power** — 1: no sign anyone pays. 3: people pay for close substitutes; the market is small or slow. 5: many buyers pay now, with money to spend, and demand is growing.
- **C02 Profit potential** — 1: thin margin, one way to earn. 3: fair margin or 2 ways to earn. 5: high margin (60%+) and 3+ ways to earn (for example ads + affiliate + product).
- **C03 Scales without your labor or face** — 1: income stops when you stop. 3: half automated; some weekly hands-on work. 5: runs as a workflow; output grows without more of your hours.
- **C04 Cost and time to first output** — 1: months and big money before the first output. 3: first output in 1–3 months within the upfront cap. 5: first output in under 2 weeks for almost nothing.
- **C05 Competition and your edge** — 1: crowded, and leaders do it better. 3: competitors exist; you have a different angle or audience. 5: a gap leaders ignore, and you have an edge they cannot copy fast.
- **C06 Doable with your skills plus AI** — 1: needs skills, licenses, or a team you lack. 3: you can learn the gaps in weeks. 5: you can start today.
- **C07 Builds your value** — 1: nothing carries over. 3: builds some skills or assets. 5: builds assets that compound (audience, data, code, brand, reputation).
- **C08 Good for people** — 1: harms or exploits. 3: neutral. 5: clearly helps people.

## Deal-breakers
A hit means Drop. The user may override with a written reason.

| ID | Deal-breaker |
|---|---|
| D01 | Needs the user to show their face |
| D02 | Needs the user's daily manual labor to earn |
| D03 | High legal risk: piracy, copied content, unlicensed regulated advice |
| D04 | Upfront cost above the cap in `owner-profile.md` |
| D05 | Cannot run as a repeatable workflow |

## Verdict bands
| Band | Minimum score |
|---|---|
| Pursue | 3.8 |
| Park | 3.0 |
| Drop | 0 |

## Caps
| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Low-confidence cap | 30 |

- If low-confidence scores carry more than this share (%) of the weight, Pursue drops to Park. Weak evidence cannot earn a Pursue.
- An `unsure` deal-breaker also caps the verdict at Park. Resolve it first.

## Quick test (stage 00 only)
Answer yes, no, or ? for each.
1. Do people already pay for something like this?
2. Can it earn without your face and without your daily labor?
3. Can you ship a first version in 30 days, within the upfront cap?
4. Do you have an edge (skill, angle, access) over the top 3 players?
5. Can it run as a repeatable workflow?
