# Idea types

One section per type. Stage 01 picks the type. Stage 02 loads **only that section**, plus "All types".

Each section lists: frame questions (stage 01), checks and sources (stage 02), ways to earn, and traps.

## All types
- **Checks:** search demand (Google Trends, search volume, Reddit and forum threads asking for it); top 3–5 competitors and their revenue signals; price points; the context scan below.
- **Context scan (opportunities):** season and weather (when demand peaks), geography (where buyers are, where it is underserved), culture and language (can it be localized?), trends (rising or fading, and why), law and policy (what could block or help it).
- **Market size:** estimate it two ways. Top-down: total buyers × share you can reach. Bottom-up: price × buyers you can reach in month 12. Mark both as `(estimate)`.
- **Google Trends:** charts often do not load through a fetch tool. Search for articles that quote the trend, or ask the user to paste a screenshot.

## T1 Digital product (templates, ebook, cheat sheet, printable, wallpaper, infographic)
- **Frame:** Who buys it, and what job does it do for them? One product or a product line?
- **Checks:** best-seller lists and review counts on Etsy, Gumroad, Creative Market, and Amazon KDP; prices; how many near-copies exist.
- **Earn:** one-time sales, bundles, a membership, upsell to a course.
- **Traps:** a race to the bottom on price; AI makes copies cheap. The edge must be the niche or the distribution, not the file.

## T2 App or software (mobile, web, SaaS, tool)
- **Frame:** Which painful, frequent task does it fix? Who pays: users, businesses, or advertisers?
- **Checks:** App Store and Google Play rankings, ratings, and complaints in reviews (gaps); Product Hunt; G2 or Capterra for B2B; public download estimates (Sensor Tower, AppMagic); open-source rivals on GitHub.
- **Earn:** subscription, freemium, one-time, B2B license, ads.
- **Traps:** building before proof of demand; store fees (15–30%); support load; data-privacy law.

## T3 Content channel (YouTube, blog, newsletter, short video)
- **Frame:** What is the one thing the channel does? Faceless format? How often can the workflow publish?
- **Checks:** top channels in the niche on Social Blade (growth, views per video); views versus subscriber count on recent videos (a sign of demand); ad rate (CPM) range for the niche; affiliate programs and payouts; sponsor fit.
- **Earn:** ads, affiliate links, sponsors, merch, own products, a funnel to a site or list.
- **Traps:** months before payout (YouTube Partner Program needs 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours, or 10 M Shorts views in 90 days); platform rule changes; mass-produced AI content may be demonetized.

## T4 Book, course, or study guide
- **Frame:** Which reader, at which moment, with which outcome? Is it evergreen or tied to an exam or tool version?
- **Checks:** Amazon best-seller rank (BSR) and review counts; Udemy and Coursera enrollments; exam pass rates and test-taker numbers (for professional guides); communities that ask for the material.
- **Earn:** sales, cohort or course fees, B2B team licenses, updates by subscription.
- **Traps:** the content goes out of date; copyright of source material; marketing is most of the work.

## T5 Physical business or franchise (café, shop, service location)
- **Frame:** Owner-operator or investor? Which location? What can be automated or delegated?
- **Checks:** franchise disclosure documents (FDD, United States) or the local equivalent; rent, wages, and fit-out costs for the city; foot traffic and competitors on Google Maps; local permits; failure rates for the sector.
- **Earn:** margin per unit, more locations, franchise fees.
- **Traps:** high upfront cost (often hits D04); daily labor (D02); local regulation. Usually needs an operator to pass the hard noes.

## T6 Marketing campaign or service (agency, done-for-you, lead generation)
- **Frame:** Whose problem, and how is success measured? Can the delivery be an ICM pipeline?
- **Checks:** what clients pay now (Upwork, Fiverr, agency price pages); how clients find vendors; proof that the result can be measured.
- **Earn:** retainer, per-project fee, revenue share, productized service.
- **Traps:** turns into client labor (D02); churn; hard to scale without hiring.

## T7 Game or simulation
- **Frame:** Genre, platform, session length. What makes people share it?
- **Checks:** SteamDB and Steam tags (review counts × about 30–60 ≈ sales `(estimate)`); itch.io; mobile game charts; top creators who would play it.
- **Earn:** premium sale, in-app purchases, ads, a sponsored or educational version.
- **Traps:** a hit-driven market; long build time; discovery is the hard part.
